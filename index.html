1. About this policy

This policy describes how Pin Rush handles data.

The full app title is Pin Rush: Infinite Bowling.

Robert Baldwin operates the app.

This page is the current public privacy policy.

2. Who this policy covers

This policy covers people who use the Android app Pin Rush.

It applies in all countries where the app is available.

3. What the app does

Pin Rush is a free casual arcade bowling game.

The ball moves forward on its own.

You steer left and right.

You can watch interstitial ads after some runs.

You can buy a one-time Remove Ads purchase.

Pin Rush includes an optional All-Time top 10 distance Hall of Fame.

You do not need an account to play.

4. Summary of data practices

  • The app keeps gameplay and settings data on your device.
  • After each online run, the app may send a random player ID and your run distance to check Hall of Fame qualification.
  • Only top-10 Hall of Fame entries are stored online and shown publicly (display name and distance).
  • Do not use your real name on the public board if you do not want it shown to others.
  • Google AdMob may collect and share ad-related data to show ads.
  • Google Play Billing handles Remove Ads purchases.
  • We do not run a separate first-party analytics or crash service in the current app build.

5. Data on the device

The app stores data in local app storage on your device.

This local data can include:

  • your best distance score
  • music, sound, vibration, units, and language settings
  • your selected cosmetic ball
  • a first-run tip flag
  • Remove Ads ownership state
  • ad timing counters used only on the device
  • a random player ID for the Hall of Fame
  • your saved Hall of Fame display name, if you entered one

Most settings and your local best stay on the device only.

When you are online and the Hall of Fame is enabled, the app may send your player ID and run distance after each run to check qualification.

The app sends your display name only when you provide it (for example when a run qualifies).

Uninstall of the app, or clear of app storage, removes this local data.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Pin Rush can show full-screen interstitial ads through Google AdMob.

Google and its advertising partners may collect and use data such as:

  • Advertising ID and other device or app identifiers
  • IP address (this can support approximate location)
  • app interaction data related to ads
  • diagnostic data about ad and SDK performance

Google may use this data for advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention.

See Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can review Google ad settings here: https://adssettings.google.com/

Personalized ads can depend on your region, your device settings, and Google tools.

Where law requires consent for certain ad processing, Google or system tools may collect that consent.

This build does not show a separate Pin Rush in-app consent form for ads.

7. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Remove Ads is a one-time in-app purchase through Google Play.

Google Play processes payment data.

We do not receive your full payment card numbers.

We receive purchase or entitlement state so the app can remove ads and restore the purchase.

8. Online Hall of Fame

Pin Rush includes a live All-Time top 10 distance Hall of Fame.

How it works:

  • Only the current top 10 distances are stored online.
  • You do not need an account to play or view the board.
  • Offline play still works. The board and submit need Internet.
  • After each online run, the app sends your random player ID and run distance to check qualification.
  • If your run qualifies, you may enter a short display name (3–12 characters).
  • The public board shows rank, display name, and distance only.
  • Your player ID is not shown on the public board.
  • Display names on the board are public. Anyone who opens the board can see them. Do not use your real name if you do not want it shown.
  • Your display name is set when you first enter the board. Later runs can improve your distance on the board, but they do not change your display name on the server.
  • Submit traffic may use your IP address for abuse prevention and rate limits. That data is not shown on the public board.

Cloudflare (Workers and D1 database) hosts this service for us.

9. Permissions

Pin Rush is designed to use:

  • Internet access for ads, purchases, and the Hall of Fame
  • Vibration for optional haptic feedback

Pin Rush does not ask for contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location for its own game features.

10. How we use data

We use data to:

  • run the game and remember settings and best score
  • show ads unless you own Remove Ads
  • process and restore the Remove Ads purchase
  • operate the Hall of Fame (check qualification, show the board, store top-10 entries)
  • prevent abuse and apply rate limits on Hall of Fame submits
  • respond to your contact requests
  • meet legal duties when required

11. How we share data

We share data as follows:

  • with Google for AdMob ads
  • with Google for Play Billing purchases
  • with Cloudflare to operate the Hall of Fame (player ID, distance, and display name when provided)
  • on the public Hall of Fame board (display name and distance for top-10 entries only)
  • with authorities when law requires it

We use service providers only as needed to run the service.

We do not sell personal data for money.

12. Sale of data and ad-related sharing

We do not sell personal data for money.

Showing your Hall of Fame display name and distance on the public top-10 board is not a sale of data.

Advertising may involve sharing of identifiers and related data with Google for ads.

In some U.S. states, that ad activity can count as a “share” or targeted advertising under local law.

You can use device ad settings and Google ad settings to limit personalized ads where those controls exist.

13. International transfers

Google may process ad and purchase data outside your country.

Cloudflare may process Hall of Fame data outside your country.

Their terms and privacy policies apply to their services.

14. Retention

  • Local app data stays on the device until you clear storage or uninstall.
  • Ad-related data follows Google’s retention practices.
  • Hall of Fame top-10 entries stay while listed or until we remove them after a valid request.

15. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect data.

Network features and SDKs use encrypted transport where the provider supports it (for example TLS).

No method is perfect. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

16. Children

Pin Rush is a general-audience casual game.

It is not directed to children under 13.

It is not enrolled as a Kids Category or Designed for Families product.

A parental gate appears before Remove Ads purchase or restore as purchase hygiene.

Parents or guardians may contact us at pinrush12@gmail.com about a child’s data.

You may ask us to remove a child’s Hall of Fame display name from the public board.

17. Rights and choices

Depending on your country or state, you may have rights to:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you
  • ask for correction
  • ask for deletion
  • object to or limit certain processing where law allows

You can also:

  • buy Remove Ads to stop interstitial ads from Pin Rush
  • use Google ad settings and Android advertising ID controls
  • uninstall the app to remove local app data
  • choose not to enter a Hall of Fame display name (you can still play)

Hall of Fame display names on the board are not changed by later runs. To change or remove a board entry, email us.

To make a request, email pinrush12@gmail.com.

Include the app name and a clear description of your request.

For Hall of Fame requests, include your display name and approximate distance. Include your player ID if you know it.

We may need enough detail to find any online record.

18. No account / how to delete data

Pin Rush does not require an account.

To delete local data: uninstall the app, or clear the app’s storage in Android settings.

To request removal of a Hall of Fame board entry: email pinrush12@gmail.com with your display name, approximate distance, and player ID if you know it. There is no in-app delete button for the online board.

Google controls deletion for data that only Google holds for ads or payments. Use Google’s tools and policies for those requests when needed.

19. Legal bases (EEA, UK, and similar)

  • Consent: for certain advertising or identifier uses where consent is required

Consent, where required, may come through Google tools, system settings, or regional messaging.
